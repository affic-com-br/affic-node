# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. The summary below states what it means here; the linked text is the full document.

## Our standards

Everyone participating in this project — issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions — is expected
to:

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints, experience levels, and native languages.
- Give and accept feedback about the code, not about the person.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise, and move on.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment of any kind, personal or political attacks, publishing
others' private information without permission, and sexualised language or imagery.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at **contato@affic.com.br**. Reports are handled
confidentially and reviewed promptly. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge inappropriate.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face consequences decided by
the project's leadership.
